“Vitess for PostgreSQL” is a useful shorthand for the ambition behind Neki, but it significantly understates the architecture we have built. Neki carries forward what we learned from Vitess in a new sharding architecture designed around PostgreSQL’s internals and semantics from the start. PlanetScale has spent years building and operating Vitess at scale.
